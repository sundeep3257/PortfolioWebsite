##Title: AVA-TAR
##Subtitle: Using pose estimation to measure range of motion

##Category: Technology in Medicine

##Train 1: Preclinical hydrocephalus research depends on accurate measurement of ventricular morphology, but manual and atlas-based methods are often slow and inaccurate. I developed AVA-TAR to provide a more robust and standardized approach.

##Train 2: AVA-TAR uses a U-Net++ convolutional neural network to segment lateral ventricles in rat and mouse brain MRIs. Trained on hundreds of manually annotated scans, the pipeline generates 3D reconstructions and automatically measures ventricle volume, surface area, symmetry, and convexity.

##Train 3: To make the tool broadly accessible, I deployed the validated pipeline as a public website. Researchers can upload MRIs and download results within seconds, enabling faster and more reproducible analysis of hydrocephalus.
