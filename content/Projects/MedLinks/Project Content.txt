##Title: MedLinks
##Subtitle: A Connections-inspired game for medical students

##Category: Creative Projects

##Train 1: I created MedLinks as a fun way for my classmates and me to review high-yield concepts for the USMLE Step exams while testing how well we could recognize relationships across medicine.

##Train 2: Inspired by the NYT Connections format, MedLinks challenges players to sort 16 medical terms into four hidden categories. Each puzzle draws from topics across specialties, including diagnoses, medications, anatomy, and clinical management.

##Train 3: MedLinks makes studying feel more like solving a puzzle. New puzzles encourage pattern recognition, clinical associations, and friendly competition while giving us another way to prepare for our exams.
